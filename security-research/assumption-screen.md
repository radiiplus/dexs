# Assumption-Collapse Reassessment

**Review date:** 2026-11-27
**Purpose:** Re-screen completed protocol ledgers using the restoration, externally mutable state, and oracle-lineage lenses.
**Scope:** The 41 Ethereum DEX reports, six completed Ethereum lending reports, and Jupiter Lend Smart Vault operations ledger linked below. Lista Lending is still in progress and is not counted as a completed review.

## Method

The prior reports were re-read as evidence ledgers, not treated as fresh deployment audits. This pass does not refresh every pool, reserve, feed, or borrower state. It therefore makes no claim about today's exploitable market conditions.

The three lenses are kept independent:

- **A — restoration / time dependence:** Does a protocol or consumer use a time-averaged or delayed market observation? A TWAP is not automatically a spot price when restoration is weak. The price path still spans the observation window; manipulation cost depends on the actual pool curve, liquidity over the path, fee schedule, arbitrage, and observation timing. No linear restoration-cost multiplier is assumed.
- **B — externally changeable accounting input:** Which state is read by the invariant, who can change it, and how does the next state transition reconcile it? Direct token transfers, rebases, callbacks, and permissionless sync/sweep paths are considered separately. A changed balance is not itself a credit or loss; trace how the implementation converts that balance into claims or payouts.
- **C — oracle lineage:** What exact feeds or on-chain sources determine the value used for a security decision, and do they share upstream dependencies? Feed count is not independence. A source graph and configuration snapshot are required before judging correlation.

No scalar `M` is assigned. A live-market restoration estimate would require time-series and depth analysis; B requires code/state-transition evidence; C requires feed/source dependency tracing. Combining them without calibrated, compatible measurements would create false precision.

## Prioritized Follow-Up

1. **Oracle lineage across lenders:** resolve active feed/source graphs for Aave, Spark, Compound, and Venus at one pinned state. For Morpho, enumerate only markets relevant to a concrete consumer because oracle addresses are market-specific. Record shared upstreams, update cadence, fallback rules, and confidence/freshness checks. This is the most direct C-screen and does not assume manipulation is possible.
2. **Antfarm source-selection regression:** locally test the reserve-ranking and elapsed-window state transition, then measure whether the selected oracle changes an actual fee on the matching pair. Keep source selection, price movement, fee impact, and attacker cost as separate evidence gates.
3. **V3 width exposure/consumer check:** the principal truncation remains confirmed in several forks. Search wrappers and vaults for claims based on `collect` output versus raw pool balances; do not infer recoverability from the surplus itself. Deployment-specific positions at the boundary remain unestablished.
4. **TWAP restoration studies only for mapped consumers:** prioritize Uniswap V2/V3, Curve, Balancer, and any DEX observation actually consumed by a lending or settlement contract. A pool's own observation mechanism is not by itself a payout path. Use historical pool state and local simulations to estimate the exact required path; do not substitute a generic linear multiplier.
5. **Unresolved external-input paths:** retain the iZi liquidity module, SakeSwap token acceptance/value, SushiRoll deployed caller/runtime linkage, and Jupiter smart liquidation/rebalance source gap as separate evidence items. They are not made stronger merely by the new framing.

## Disposition

This reassessment does not change existing finding severities or add a confirmed vulnerability. Confirmed source/accounting defects remain confirmed at their existing scopes; the assumption-collapse lenses mainly identify where the old reports did not map downstream consumers, active feed lineage, or market-specific price restoration. The strongest evidence-backed follow-ups are Antfarm's unverified oracle-selection path, source-lineage mapping for the analyzed lenders, and deployment/consumer exposure for the confirmed V3 owed-width defects. No scalar market multiplier, current exploitable market, or profitable loop is established by this pass.
