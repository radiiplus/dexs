## Architecture and Execution Map

- `Pool` is called through the provider-owned proxy. It dispatches value operations to `SupplyLogic`, `BorrowLogic`, `LiquidationLogic`, `FlashLoanLogic`, and reserve/user accounting helpers. The provider controls implementation and Configurator references; the Configurator applies authorized reserve and risk-parameter changes.
- Each reserve tracks configuration, liquidity and variable-debt indexes, rates, scaled variable debt, accrued treasury claims, a virtual underlying balance, and associated aToken and variable debt token addresses. The aToken holds underlying liquidity and represents supplier claims; the variable debt token represents indexed borrower liabilities.
- Supply: validate reserve and cap; update index/treasury accrual; update rates and virtual liquidity for the incoming amount; transfer underlying to aToken; mint scaled aTokens; optionally enable collateral.
- Withdraw: calculate scaled burn amount; validate the user's scaled balance; update rates and virtual liquidity; burn aTokens and transfer underlying; update collateral flag and health-factor validation.
- Borrow: update debt index; calculate scaled debt and validate reserve status, borrow mode, borrow cap, available aToken liquidity, and user health factor; mint variable debt; update rates/virtual liquidity; transfer underlying.
- Repay: update indexes; cap payment to current debt; burn scaled variable debt; update rates and virtual liquidity; transfer underlying or burn aTokens for the aToken repayment path.
- Liquidation: update debt and collateral reserves; calculate account health and close-factor bound; calculate collateral/bonus/protocol fee with scaled-token rounding; burn/transfer debt and collateral; optionally clear collateral and burn bad debt; transfer liquidator repayment to the debt aToken.
- Flash loan: validate reserve availability and duplicate assets; transfer assets to receiver; invoke receiver callback; either collect principal plus premium or convert eligible debt-mode amounts into variable debt; update rates and virtual balance within the same transaction. Failure to settle reverts the full callback and preceding state changes.
- Additional paths reviewed: aToken transfer/finalize callbacks, `repayWithATokens`, reserve deficit elimination and bad-debt creation, eMode changes and bitmaps, position-manager delegation, liquidation grace periods, and authorized reserve configuration updates. This current implementation removed the former drop-reserve, isolation debt-ceiling, silo-borrowing, and price-oracle-sentinel paths; the remaining isolated eMode rules are explicitly represented in eMode configuration.

Active deployed source is the Blockscout-published bundle on the [Pool implementation page](https://eth.blockscout.com/address/0x4295Fe69b3e978Eb511e4558B4407AD871612fCb?tab=contract); the critical library contracts are [SupplyLogic](https://eth.blockscout.com/address/0x584C7d8c4cb05304FE5Ac7fbc97f20A10Fb07564?tab=contract), [BorrowLogic](https://eth.blockscout.com/address/0x52Da0ce88202D1542543598D1e1e27F0d344726A?tab=contract), [LiquidationLogic](https://eth.blockscout.com/address/0x96D5686812e33Ab509ECCDb38C89d15607B2a413?tab=contract), [FlashLoanLogic](https://eth.blockscout.com/address/0xC27a4d7AC0623B129919786d88714eF16132BEaE?tab=contract), and [PoolLogic](https://eth.blockscout.com/address/0xD70593841C57cbaA04957cc3EAcE95708e48853b?tab=contract). ## Critical Invariants

- For each reserve, aToken-scaled supply plus accrued treasury claims, indexed at the reserve liquidity index, represents supplier claims; variable debt is derived from scaled debt and the variable borrow index. Rounding and treasury minting must not make claims exceed collectible assets plus protocol-defined deficit treatment.
- Borrow and liquidation debt must be created or burned consistently with actual underlying inflow/outflow and the reserve's virtual liquidity update.
- A user's collateral flags, eMode rules, isolation/silo restrictions, and health factor must agree with the reserve set and indexed balances used by every borrow, withdrawal, transfer, and liquidation path.
- A liquidation's debt repayment, borrower debt burn, collateral reduction, liquidator receipt, treasury fee, and any bad-debt write-off must reconcile after scaled-token rounding.
- Flash-loan settlement must collect the exact principal and premium, or mint the corresponding allowed debt, before transaction completion; callback failure must roll back all interim state.
- Proxy implementation and market configuration changes must not leave accounting or validation logic operating against incompatible storage/state assumptions.

## Findings

No confirmed implementation defect was established in the reviewed Ethereum Pool, linked accounting libraries, Configurator, sample USDC aToken, or sample USDC variable debt-token paths. This is a scoped result, not a claim about Aave V2/V4, other chains, external oracle quality, governance decisions, or every listed token implementation.

- At block `26,492,532`, a read-only sweep of all 68 Data Provider reserve entries compared each underlying token balance held by its aToken with `Pool.getVirtualUnderlyingBalance(asset)`. Every observed cash balance was greater than or equal to its virtual-liquidity value. The positive deltas are not by themselves an accounting defect: virtual liquidity is updated by protocol operations and does not claim to include donations, rebasing/yield, or all accrued interest cash. No asset had an observed cash shortfall against this field in the sweep.
- Reserve flags at that same snapshot were heterogeneous: 36 frozen reserves coexist with 24 borrow-enabled and 55 collateral-enabled reserves. This makes reserve-list membership alone an unsafe proxy for operation eligibility; later tests must use the live configuration and user bitmap together.
- The current compiled paths use checked `SafeCast` conversions for uint128 reserve fields and virtual-balance/debt-deficit updates. Reserve caps and current configured decimals bound those quantities in the sampled deployment. No source-level truncation analogous to the reviewed V3 DEX principal-width cases was identified in the lending value paths.
- Liquidation computes account health from indexed balances, constrains close factor, calculates available collateral with explicit floor/ceil percentage rounding, tracks actual scaled collateral consumption, and clears collateral state when rounding consumes the full balance. If collateral is exhausted, the remaining debt is burned and recorded as reserve deficit; the debt reserve virtual balance increases only by liquidator-paid debt. No inconsistent before/after accounting sequence was established in the reviewed code.
- Flash loan execution pre-decrements virtual liquidity before transferring funds to the receiver, runs the callback, then either pulls principal plus rounded premium or invokes normal borrow validation for debt-mode settlement. All these steps share one transaction, so failed callback/settlement or post-callback health checks roll back preceding state updates. Duplicate assets are rejected. A longer `interestRateModes` array is ignored beyond `assets.length`; a shorter one reverts on indexed access. This length asymmetry does not create a value-accounting bypass in the reviewed flow.
- `repayWithATokens` decreases debt and burns the caller's matching aToken value; because no underlying enters or leaves the reserve, it leaves virtual liquidity unchanged. Its health-factor validation and collateral-bit clearing operate on the post-burn state.

These are source/runtime observations, not final classifications. The reserve sweep is one state snapshot and is not a substitute for operation-sequence tests.

## Rejected Hypotheses

- **Cash above virtual liquidity is a reserve-accounting shortfall:** disproven for the observed snapshot. All 68 underlying balances were at least their virtual balances; positive deltas are consistent with untracked donations and cash from accrued interest/repayment. They are not proof that each reserve's full supplier claim is solvent.
- **The public `aave-v3-origin` repository snapshot is the active deployed source:** disproven for the current Pool implementation. Its local build differs in runtime size from the deployed implementation. The 44-file explorer-published source bundle, using Blockscout's recorded settings and library addresses, instead compiles exactly to the active runtime.
- **aToken and debt-token rounding directions alone demonstrate value leakage:** disproven as a standalone claim. `TokenMath` documents mint-floor/burn-ceil for aTokens and mint-ceil/burn-floor for variable debt, with scaled balances converted in opposite conservative directions. A concrete sequence would be needed to show an invariant failure; none has been established in this pass.
- **A reserve-list gap makes eMode changes revert:** disproven for the sampled Ethereum deployment. `getReservesList()` returned 68 contiguous nonzero entries at IDs 0 through 67. The active v3.7 implementation has removed the `dropReserve` entrypoint; legacy gap checks remain in account calculation and bad-debt iteration, but no gap exists in the observed state.
- **Liquidation ceil rounding can strand debt after all collateral shares are consumed:** addressed by the active implementation's scaled-consumption check and deficit path. Upstream v3.7 regression cases exercise single-debt, multi-debt, and sum-of-independent-ceils boundaries in `Pool.Liquidations.t.sol`; these repository tests were inspected but not run against the deployment fork. No counterexample was established in the deployed source review.
- **A reserve-listed token with transfer-tax behavior can make requested supply exceed received underlying:** the Pool does account requested amounts and does not measure balance deltas. No confirmed active Ethereum reserve using transfer-tax semantics was identified in this pass; the reserve listing process and supported-token behavior remain an explicit token-compatibility assumption, not a confirmed exploit finding.

## Coverage and Limits

- Reviewed deployed source transitions for supply/withdraw, borrow/repay, aToken transfer validation, liquidation and bad-debt creation, flash loans, reserve indexes/treasury accrual/virtual liquidity, eMode and position-manager configuration, deficit coverage, and reserve configuration authorization.
- Recompiled the active Pool, Configurator, aToken, and variable debt-token source bundles; checked linked logic library runtime bytes against their explorer-published deployed bytecodes; verified the address book/provider pointers and sampled reserve configuration.
- Fork validation was read-only at block `26,492,532`. No complete user-operation sequence was submitted on the local fork because the source review produced no credible candidate requiring reproduction. Upstream property/regression tests were reviewed but not executed.
- External oracle correctness/freshness, governance/configuration choices, per-asset issuer controls, downstream integrators, and every token's issuer-side upgradeability are not findings in this ledger. The code-level assumption that listed underlyings transfer the requested amount remains a market-listing compatibility condition.
