# Maple (Ethereum) Evidence Ledger

## Status and Scope

Status: **analyzed**. Maple is multi-chain and has many Ethereum pools. This ledger uses the largest documented Ethereum lending product, syrupUSDC, to assess the shared Maple Pool/PoolManager/withdrawal-manager design and its active open-term loan accounting. It does not claim to audit every Maple pool, every borrower loan, the strategy implementations, Maple's other chains, or off-chain collateral custody and legal enforcement.

## Architecture and Execution Map

- Pool is the ERC-20 share token. Its `deposit`, `mint`, `redeem`, and request-exit methods call PoolManager; `checkCall` asks the pool's permission manager whether the user/receiver may perform the action. It uses a reentrancy lock for deposit, mint, redeem, withdraw, and request-exit flows. Direct `withdraw` is disabled in the active manager; liquidity exits use the queue.
- PoolManager owns pool configuration and delegates accounting to active strategies. It controls pool delegate and pending delegate, active/configured state, liquidity and strategy caps, withdrawal manager, and permission manager. Protocol/operational admins, governor, pool delegate, security admin, and MapleGlobals have different controls.
- `Pool.totalAssets()` delegates to `PoolManager.totalAssets()`. The manager adds USDC held by the Pool plus each direct strategy's `assetsUnderManagement()`. For an entry marked as a StrategyManager, it instead adds `assetsOf(poolManager)`. This distinction is required for correct reconciliation.
- Lender deposit: permission check, PoolManager cap/active/allowlist checks, share preview rounded down, mint shares, and transfer USDC into Pool. Initial supply uses the Pool's immutable `BOOTSTRAP_MINT` (100,000 raw units in this deployment) to make zero-address bootstrap shares.
- Strategy/loan funding: the PoolManager checks that the caller is a registered strategy/manager and enforces the strategy cap, then moves assets from Pool cash to the strategy destination. The OpenTermLoanManager's `fund` path is pool-delegate-only, validates the approved loan factory and borrower, gets funds through PoolManager, calls the loan's `fund`, updates `principalOut`, and records scheduled interest issuance.
- Loan payment: borrower pays principal, scheduled interest, late interest, and fees to the loan; the loan calls the LoanManager `claim`, which validates remaining-principal/due-date consistency, removes impairment accounting if relevant, distributes the funds to the Pool, delegate, and treasury, and updates principal and interest accounting.
- Default: pool delegate, governor, or operational admin can ask PoolManager to trigger default. LoanManager accounts for impairment, repossesses the loan's configured funds/collateral, distributes recoveries, realizes remaining losses, and removes the loan's payment schedule. Default and collateral enforcement can involve off-chain custody and are outside the on-chain invariant claims here.
- Exit request: a lender's shares move to PoolManager escrow and WithdrawalManagerQueue tracks the request. A redeemer processes requests. Queue calculations use `totalAssets - unrealizedLosses` and USDC cash held by Pool. When automatic redemption is selected, the queue calls Pool.redeem with itself as share owner; PoolManager calls queue `processExit`, which recognizes the queue as `owner == address(this)`, and Pool burns escrowed shares while USDC goes to the lender. Manual exits leave shares available to the owner until redeemable.
- Partial queue processing computes `requiredLiquidity = floor(netAssets * shares / totalSupply)`, then limits redeemable shares by `floor(shares * availableLiquidity / requiredLiquidity)` when cash is insufficient. Resulting assets are rounded down. Unprocessed shares remain in the request; processed shares are removed from the queue's accounting.
- SyrupRouter performs allowlist authorization and deposits into the same Pool. It does not hold a separate lending balance sheet.

## Critical Invariants

- Pool assets must equal idle underlying cash plus each direct strategy's recognized AUM and each StrategyManager's pool-specific asset amount. The value used for share conversion must use the same strategy classification as the manager's cash-out and cap logic.
- Deposits mint shares no greater than the proportional value received; redemptions burn the exact escrowed shares paid out, and output assets must not exceed Pool cash at the moment of processing.
- Queue `totalShares`, per-user escrow, request shares, manual available shares, and PoolManager-held escrowed shares must move together through request, partial processing, cancellation/removal, manual redemption, and final settlement.
- Loan principal out, accounted interest, accrued interest, repayment/fee transfers, impairment losses, collateral recoveries, and default write-offs must reconcile with strategy AUM and exit haircuts.
- Loan funding and default/impairment transitions must be limited to the configured manager, pool delegate, governor, operational/security admins, and approved factories as the source specifies.
- Permission checks and transaction rollback must prevent an unsuccessful pool or loan operation from changing shares, queue state, accounting, or token balances.

## Findings

No confirmed implementation defect was established in the analyzed syrupUSDC Pool, its active PoolManager and WithdrawalManagerQueue implementations, or the active OpenTermLoanManager accounting paths.

- At pinned block `26,492,755`, PoolManager was active/configured, liquidity cap was `2,500,000,000,000,000` raw USDC units, seven strategy entries were listed, and unrealized losses were zero. Pool total supply was `515,455,652,806,578` raw shares; total assets were `615,914,638,855,263` raw USDC units; Pool-held cash was `14,233,773,044,052`.
- The PoolManager asset calculation reconciled exactly at that block: Pool cash `14,233,773,044,052` + OpenTermLoanManager AUM `601,672,457,306,685` + StrategyManager pool assets `4,018,997,699` + `4,389,506,827` = Pool total assets `615,914,638,855,263`. StrategyManagers with no pool allocation contributed zero through `assetsOf(poolManager)`; their separate `assetsUnderManagement()` sentinel values are not included by PoolManager. No stale strategy value or cash/accounting mismatch was observed in this check.
- OpenTermLoanManager's AUM is `principalOut + accountedInterest + accruedInterest`; the impairment path removes future issuance and adds principal plus accrued scheduled interest to unrealized losses. Default processing then reconciles recovered funds and remaining losses before removing payment accounting. This sequence is internally consistent in the reviewed source; no candidate required mutation on a local fork.
- The withdrawal queue's partial-liquidity path floors both redeemed shares and resulting assets. Since required liquidity is the floor of the full share value, partial redemption output cannot exceed the available cash under the implemented integer arithmetic. Queue and Pool share custody also align: the WithdrawalManagerQueue is the actual owner of escrowed shares when automatic Pool redemption is invoked.
- `withdraw` and `requestWithdraw` are deliberately disabled; lenders use queued `requestRedeem`/`redeem`. This is an expected interface limitation, not an accounting defect.

## Rejected Hypotheses

- **Pool cash below totalAssets means assets are unaccounted:** disproven for the pinned snapshot. The difference is explained by OpenTermLoanManager AUM plus StrategyManager pool allocations; the sum reconciles exactly.
- **StrategyManager `assetsUnderManagement` must be included in Pool NAV:** disproven. PoolManager branches on `isStrategyManager` and uses `assetsOf(poolManager)`, avoiding cross-pool aggregation and excluding non-pool sentinel AUM.
- **Partial withdrawal calculation can pay more than available cash because required liquidity rounds down:** disproven by the integer bounds in `_calculateRedemption`; output is also rounded down, and the partial branch is only used when available liquidity is less than the floored required amount.
- **Queue processing burns a lender's wallet shares rather than manager-held escrow:** disproven by call argument composition. The queue invokes Pool.redeem with itself as the share owner, while the lender is the receiver; PoolManager then routes `processExit` back to the queue's internal redemption calculation.
- **Impairment immediately reduces total assets and then unrealized losses double-count it:** not established. LoanManager's `assetsUnderManagement` and separate `unrealizedLosses` are designed so the Pool's exit value subtracts the temporarily impaired principal and interest until recovery/default accounting resolves it. No nonzero impairment was observed in this snapshot.
- **An ordinary donation to Pool creates free shares:** not established. Donations increase the value used for all shares; initial supply also uses a bootstrap mint. No profitable multi-operation share inflation path was found in the reviewed code.

## Validation and Limits

- Read-only Ethereum RPC verified the Pool/Manager/WithdrawalManager/LoanManager relationships and manager implementation addresses. State reconciliation used an explicit block tag so the cash, AUM, total assets, and total supply reads share one snapshot.
- Blockscout source bundles were compiled using their recorded compiler settings. The four runtime comparisons match executable bytes after immutable substitution; the only differences were CBOR metadata bytes. Current public source repositories were separately pinned and linked above.
- No credible invariant-violation candidate warranted a state-mutating fork regression. No live transaction was submitted.
- This review samples syrupUSDC, not all Maple pools. External collateral custody, borrower identity/credit, strategy underlying protocols, oracle valuation inside strategies, permission-admin governance, emergency intervention, token behavior, and off-chain recovery are outside the confirmed-finding scope.
